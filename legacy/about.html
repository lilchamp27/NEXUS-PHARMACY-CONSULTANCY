About us

The connection between the script and the cupboard.

Nexus exists because the most important medication information in a patient's life isn't in a file. It's at home, and someone has to go and look.

Our purpose

Medication safety, one household at a time.

We partner with general practice, aged care providers and community pharmacy to deliver credentialed medication reviews that are thorough, unhurried and genuinely useful to the person who has to take the medicines.

The name is the idea: a nexus is a connection point. A medication review only works when the prescriber, the pharmacist, the carer and the patient are all looking at the same accurate picture — and our job is to build that picture and hand it back to the people who can act on it.

How we work

Principles, not slogans.

Credentialed clinicians only

Reviews are conducted by pharmacists credentialed to perform them — never delegated, never desk-based.

The patient is the expert

Nobody knows more about how a regimen actually plays out than the person living with it. We start by listening.

Reports people read

Clear, prioritised and short enough to be read between patients — because a report that isn't read changes nothing.

Privacy as standard

Health information is handled in line with Australian privacy law, shared only with the people the patient has agreed to.

Responsive turnaround

Referrals triaged the day they arrive, with post-discharge patients prioritised ahead of the queue.

Continuity, not transactions

With consent, the plan goes to the patient's regular pharmacy so the work doesn't stop at our report.
